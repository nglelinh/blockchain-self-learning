# Lecture: MEV - Maximal Extractable Value

## 1. Concept Overview

MEV (Maximal Extractable Value, formerly "Miner Extractable Value") đại diện cho profit mà validators/miners có thể extract thông qua ability reorder, include, hoặc exclude transactions trong blocks they produce. Phenomenon này arose từ transparent mempool của Ethereum và deterministic execution của smart contracts, enabling sophisticated actors preview transaction effects và manipulate ordering for profit, often at expense của regular users.

MEV discovered và formalized bởi **Phil Daian và colleagues** trong paper "Flash Boys 2.0" năm 2019, drawing parallels với high-frequency trading trong traditional finance. Research showed billions of dollars extracted annually through techniques như front-running, back-running, và sandwich attacks, raising concerns về fairness, decentralization, và protocol stability.

---

## 2. Intuitive Understanding

MEV comparable to insider trading trên stock market. Imagine seeing everyone's orders before they execute, ability prioritize your own trades, và capacity rearrange order flow for maximum personal profit. Traditional finance heavily regulates này. Blockchain mempool inherently transparent - all pending transactions visible - creating MEV opportunities structurally embedded trong protocol design.

## 3. Technical Foundation

Sandwich attack exemplifies MEV extraction. Bot detects large DEX trade trong mempool, front-runs bằng cách buying before victim (increasing price), victim's trade executes at worse price, bot back-runs selling at profit.

```python
class MEVBot:
    def detect_opportunity(self, pending_tx):
        if pending_tx['type'] == 'DEX_SWAP' and pending_tx['amount'] > threshold:
            # Large trade detected - sandwich opportunity
            return self.execute_sandwich(pending_tx)
```

## 4. Mathematical / Cryptographic Formulation

MEV extraction quantified as:

\[
\text{MEV} = \max_{o \in O} R(o) - R(o_{\text{default}})
\]

Where \(O\) = set của possible transaction orderings, \(R(o)\) = revenue under ordering \(o\).

Sandwich attack profit:

\[
\Pi = (P_{\text{after}} - P_{\text{before}}) \times Q - \text{Gas}
\]

Example: Victim buys 100 ETH on Uniswap, bot sandwiches:
- Front-run 50 ETH: Price $2000 → $2020
- Victim 100 ETH: Price $2020 → $2060  
- Back-run 50 ETH: Sell at $2060

Profit: 50 × ($2060 - $2000) - gas ≈ $3,000

## 5. Implementation Insight

**Flashbots** mitigates MEV through sealed-bid auctions:

```python
# Instead of public mempool, private transaction bundles
bundle = {
    'transactions': [front_run_tx, victim_tx, back_run_tx],
    'block_number': target_block,
    'bribe': 0.1 ETH  # Paid to validator
}

# Submit directly to validator
# No public mempool exposure
# Reduces harmful MEV (though doesn't eliminate)
```

## 6. Common Challenges / Attacks / Trade-offs

MEV creates centralization pressure - sophisticated infrastructure required, favoring large operators. Estimated $600M+ extracted annually, mostly by small number of entities. Threatens decentralization và fairness.

---

## 7. Related Concepts

**MEV-Boost** enables validators outsource block building to specialized builders, separating proposing from building. Increases MEV extraction efficiency whilst maintaining decentralization of proposal rights.

## 10. Summary

MEV represents billions extracted through transaction ordering manipulation. While some MEV unavoidable (arbitrage beneficial for efficiency), harmful MEV (sandwich attacks) damages user experience và fairness. Solutions like Flashbots, encrypted mempools, và fair ordering protocols attempt mitigate whilst preserving validator economics. Understanding MEV essential for protocol designers, traders, và anyone interacting with DeFi.
